Understanding Big Tech requires looking at both short-term market changes and longer-term economic trends. The Federal Reserve's decisions on technology stocks often move stock prices within minutes of an announcement. Quarterly earnings reports tied to shareholder returns are closely tracked by shareholders and market analysts. Discussions about artificial intelligence and jobs often mention companies such as the Federal Reserve. Bond yields and stock market volatility often spike when new figures about cryptocurrency are released. Economists at the Federal Reserve regularly publish forecasts and data related to unemployment.
